## 1. Overview

**[Operator legal name]** (the “Operator”) respects your privacy and aims to comply with applicable data-protection laws. KnitPeek is a **local-first** web application for viewing knitting patterns and keeping personal project records.

Under the currently documented core functionality, you do not need to register an account, and imported PDF or photo patterns and work records are not uploaded to an Operator-controlled server or synchronized to an Operator account. **Separately from local document processing, GitHub Pages, the hosting provider, records visitor IP addresses for security purposes.**

## 2. Categories of information and purposes

| Category | Data | Purpose and method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Locally stored project data | Imported PDFs and photos, project names and tags, cover images, annotations, progress lines, charts, counter/timer records, reports, and notes | Display, editing, restoration, and export in browser storage. No automatic upload of imported files to an Operator server under the documented core functionality |
| Local preferences | Language choice, viewer settings, project state, and related preferences | Remember your settings on your device. Browser language settings may be read to choose an initial interface language |
| Hosting access information | Visitor IP address and related information needed to deliver and secure the website | GitHub Pages processes hosting/security data under its applicable policies |
| Voluntary support requests, if a channel is operated | Email address, the message you send, and details necessary to respond | Respond to your inquiry, **if the Operator provides a support channel** |

**Important:** A pattern or note stored only on your device may contain personal information, but that does not mean the Operator receives or has access to its contents. Information you independently export or share may be handled by the destination you select.

## 3. Retention

1. **Local project data:** Remains in the relevant device/browser storage until you delete it or the browser/device removes it. It is not a separate database of user files maintained by the Operator.
2. **Local preferences:** May remain in browser storage until deleted or reset.
3. **GitHub Pages access logs:** Retained by GitHub under its own practices for security and service operation. The Operator does not set the individual retention or deletion schedule for those logs. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
4. **Support inquiries:** If support is offered, information may be retained only as necessary to respond, manage related issues, and meet legal obligations, then deleted. **Actual retention schedule: [Enter the Operator’s policy].**

## 5. Cookies, browser storage, and similar technologies

1. KnitPeek uses **browser storage such as IndexedDB** to remember projects and settings on your device. Browser storage is not, by itself, a mechanism for uploading those contents to a server.
2. The supplied architecture documents do not identify an advertising or visitor analytics integration. **If the deployed site uses advertising tags, analytics, error-reporting tools, or other third-party scripts**, this Policy must be updated to identify relevant providers, data categories, purposes, retention, cookies, and consent or opt-out controls before those features are enabled where required.
3. You may clear or restrict cookies and site data through your browser. Clearing site data can also remove stored patterns and work records. First check that the original files and report outputs you need are stored separately.
4. Moving from a `github.io` website origin to `knitpeek.com` does **not** automatically move existing IndexedDB data to the new origin. Check that you have separately kept the materials you need before changing addresses.

## 6. Exports, sharing, and external websites

1. When you choose to export or share documents, reports, or project records, the selected information may be transferred to the storage destination or third-party sharing service you choose.
2. Following a URL or QR code in a pattern opens an external website, which may collect access data under its own privacy policy.
3. These user-initiated actions do not mean that KnitPeek automatically publishes or uploads your original patterns.

## 7. Deletion

1. You can remove locally stored project information through available deletion features or by clearing site data in your browser. Any backup you exported separately must be managed and deleted separately by you.
2. If the Operator holds support messages or other personal information, it will delete them when no longer necessary and when no legal retention requirement applies, using reasonable deletion methods.
3. GitHub processes and deletes any access-related records it holds under its own rules and procedures.

## 8. Your rights and choices

1. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal data actually held or processed by the Operator.
2. Submit requests to **[Privacy contact email]**. The Operator will respond in accordance with applicable law.
3. Where your project data exists only on your device, the Operator cannot remotely access, correct, restore, or delete that data. You can manage it using your own device and the Service’s local features.
4. Where the EU/EEA GDPR or another privacy law applies, additional rights may include data portability, objection, withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent, and the right to complain to a competent data-protection authority, subject to legal conditions.

## 9. Security measures

1. The Service is designed to process imported patterns and project records in your browser, reducing unnecessary transmission of that material to an Operator-controlled server.
2. HTTPS is used for website delivery where supported and configured.
3. You should protect your device, keep your browser updated, and store exported backups securely.
4. Local-first storage does not mean that your device’s storage is automatically encrypted or immune to data loss or unauthorized access.

## 10. Children’s privacy

KnitPeek is not designed exclusively for any specific age group. The documented core Service does not create user accounts or collect dates of birth. If features involving personal data from children are introduced, the Operator will implement any parental-consent and related safeguards required by applicable law.
